Music Hub

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Music Hub does not collect, sell or share your personal data, and contains no advertising or analytics.

What stays on your iPhone

Your name and photo, listening history, podcast subscriptions, playback positions and downloads, imported music, mixes and settings are stored only on your device (and in your device backup).

iCloud

Pins of Apple Music albums, playlists and artists are kept in your private iCloud storage so they appear on your other devices signed in with the same Apple ID. Only the pin list is stored there (names and Apple Music IDs). The developer cannot see it. Pins of your own library and imported files stay on the device.

Services the app talks to

To play and find content the app connects directly to the services you use: Apple Music (through Apple's MusicKit), Apple's podcast directory and Podcast Index for podcast search, and the podcast feeds you subscribe to and the servers that host their episodes. These services receive the technical data needed to deliver audio, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Apple Music

With your permission the app reads your Apple Music library and plays music through Apple's MusicKit. Mixes, artist information and similar artists come from the Apple Music catalog. When you save a mix, the app creates a playlist in your Apple Music library; nothing is added there otherwise. Apple handles this data under Apple's privacy policy; the app does not send your library or history anywhere else.

Camera and photos

Used only when you choose a profile picture. The picture stays on your device.

Children

Music Hub does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored. Downloads and cached images can also be removed in Settings → Storage. iCloud pins can be removed by unpinning them, or in your device's iCloud settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
