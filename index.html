Music Hub

Support

Music Hub brings your Apple Music library, daily mixes, live radio, podcasts and your own imported music together in one player.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: musichubapp@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. You can also write from the app: Settings → Contact Support.

Common questions

Do I need Apple Music? Mixes, live radio and the Apple Music catalog need an Apple Music subscription. Imported music and podcasts work without it.

How do I add my own music? Library → Imported → Import from Files, or copy files to Music Hub in Finder on a Mac. Pull down in Imported to refresh.

Why don't all my pins show on my iPad? Only pins of Apple Music albums, playlists and artists sync through iCloud. Pins of imported music stay on the device that has the files.
